### Major

**PER-F01: on a weekend, the room-week view opens on the finished week, and "Sonraki hafta" skips the coming week** (major, CRBS path)

- **Repro:**
  1. Sign in as `personel.test` on Sunday 18 Oct.
  2. Rezervasyonlar → "Salona göre".
  3. The grid shows Pzt 12 – Cum 16 Eki, every cell unbookable.
  4. Click "Sonraki hafta": URL `date=2026-10-26`, grid 26–30 Eki. The week of 19–23 Oct is never shown.
  - Same on the phone.
- **Expected:** on a non-teaching day the view opens on the coming week, and next/previous move one calendar week.
- **Cause:** `services/bookings.py:2127-2128` (`_prev_next`) for week display uses `target ± 7 days`, where target is the Sunday. The first open date ≥ 25 Oct is Mon 26.
- **Fix:**
  - For week display, compute from the Monday of `target`'s week: `after = [d … if d >= monday(target) + 7]`, `before = [d … if d < monday(target)]`.
  - When `target` is a day without periods, open the next week that has one.
- **Check:** with `date=2026-10-18&display=room`, `nav.next == 2026-10-19`.
