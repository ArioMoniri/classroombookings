# WG-10: welcome guide persona runs (spec §15), lane wg10-cal10, 2026-10-18

## 1. BLUF

- **Findings:** blocker 0, major 2, minor 9.
- **Verdict:** the welcome guide works end to end for the academician, the staff/approver and the admin, in Turkish, on desktop and phone. Two defects matter:
  - the admin's "show again" ("Güncellendi") badge flashes for about 250 ms and disappears (the wg-admin spec fails on it);
  - focus falls to `<body>` on the interactive tour step and after a sheet card navigates.
- **Stack:** gracious (API 19900 / web 19901, `next start`), commit `93cfd24`.

## 2. Setup and method

- **Viewports:** desktop 1440×900 with the mouse and keyboard; phone 390×844 with touch (isMobile, tr-TR, Europe/Istanbul). One browser at a time, under `nice`.
- **Accounts:** all made by hand in /admin/users (Yeni kullanıcı) as `admin@smartsched.local`, with the persona password from the env file.

  | Account | Persona | How it was set up |
  |---|---|---|
  | `wg.101` | Ayla, academician | Öğretim elemanı, Psikoloji. Linked in the user dialog › Öğretim elemanı kaydı › Bağla to instructor 126 "Prof. Dr. İ. U." (PSK courses). Its first sign-in was a real first sign-in on the phone (`last_login_at` was null). |
  | `wg.102` | Pınar, staff | Unlinked Öğretim elemanı. A transient sign-in timeout cut off its first run after the first sheet and the first "Sonra". |
  | `wg.103` | Selin, staff | Unlinked Öğretim elemanı. The full staff run, then the approver grant. |
  | `wg.104` | Deniz, academician | Linked to instructor 237 "Doç. Dr. S. T. K.". Used for the admin switch and text tests. |

- **By hand vs API:** every step was clicked or tapped in the UI. The API was used only to read `GET /welcome`, `/welcome/settings` and `/welcome/stats` as verification, and for the cleanup at the end.
- **Sign-in timeouts:** the shared stack sometimes needed a second sign-in attempt (30 s timeouts). This was retried and is not counted as a finding.
- **Specs:** wg-sheet, wg-tour, wg-checklist, wg-hints and wg-admin were run with E2E_REAL=1 (results in §3 and in the lane's e2e-results table). They create throw-away `wg.*.<stamp>` accounts with the spec's fixture password.
- **Accessibility:** aria snapshots via Playwright `locator.ariaSnapshot()`, and axe-core 4 injected from `node_modules/axe-core`.
- **Reduced motion and transparency:** reduced motion via the context `reducedMotion: "reduce"`. Reduced transparency via CDP `Emulation.setEmulatedMedia` with `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`.

## 4. Findings

### WG10-F01 · "Güncellendi" badge disappears about 250 ms after the sheet opens · MAJOR
- **Repro:**
  1. As `admin@smartsched.local`, open /admin/welcome?tab=texts › Öğretim elemanı and change the TR title.
  2. Click Kaydet, tick "Evet, 'Güncellendi' etiketiyle", then Kaydet.
  3. Sign in as an academician who had completed the sheet (here `wg.101`, or the spec's `wg.adm.old.*`).
- **Observed:** the sheet opens with the new title. The badge `welcome-updated` renders and is removed after about 250 ms. Trace of the spec run:
  - GET /welcome `show.updated=true` (12:59:44.114);
  - POST /welcome/events `{"event":"shown"}` (45.141);
  - GET /welcome `show=null, sheet.updated=false` (45.398).

  The badge goes because the model is rebuilt from the refetched state. `e2e/wg-admin.spec.ts:97` fails on this.
- **Expected:** the badge stays for the whole life of that sheet (spec §6.1 T4, §15.7).
- **Where:**
  - `frontend/src/components/welcome/welcome-provider.tsx:86-87` (opens, then posts `shown`);
  - `frontend/src/lib/api/welcome.ts:285` (invalidates the state);
  - `frontend/src/components/welcome/model.ts:85` (`updated: state.sheet.updated || !!state.show?.updated`, read live);
  - `backend/app/services/welcome.py:784` (`shown` stores the seen versions).
- **Fix:** carry `updated` in the sheet request at open time (`openSheet({ mode: "auto", kind, deltaAudiences, updated: !!show.updated })`) and let `buildSheet` use `req.updated` instead of the live query. Alternatively, do not invalidate `welcomeKeys.state` for the `shown` event.

### WG10-F02 · Focus falls to `<body>` in two welcome actions · MAJOR
- **Repro (a), the interactive tour step:**
  1. As `wg.101` at 1440, open the user menu › Turu yeniden başlat › Kısa turu başlat.
  2. Press Enter on İleri.

  At step 2 "Önce zamanı seçin" (`reserve-when`, the cross-route step to /reserve), `document.activeElement` is `<body>`. Tab then goes to page buttons, not to the card's Geri/İleri. The same happens at 390. A screen-reader user hears nothing at this step, and a keyboard user has to tab through the page to find İleri.
- **Repro (b), a sheet card:** as `wg.103` at 1440, open Yardım › Turu yeniden başlat and click the "Salon bul" card. You are on /find-room with focus on `<body>`.
- **Expected:** focus is never on `<body>` after an action (brief rule). Spec §4.1: after a card, focus goes to the page's h1. Spec §12: interactive steps let Tab reach the target and come back.
- **Where:**
  - (a) `frontend/src/components/welcome/tour/joyride-adapter.tsx:77` (`disableFocusTrap: step.interactive`) with nothing that moves focus into the new card;
  - (b) `frontend/src/components/welcome/welcome-provider.tsx:149-150`: `focusPageHeading(3000)` runs right after `router.push`, finds the old page's `main h1` (`welcome-store.ts:87-91`), focuses it and stops. Then that h1 unmounts.
- **Fix:**
  - (a) on every step start (interactive or not), focus the card's İleri button (or the card itself), and leave Tab free to reach the target;
  - (b) in `focusPageHeading`, remember the h1 node found before navigation and wait for a different `main h1` (or for `pathname` to change) before focusing.

### WG10-F03 · Phone checklist stays stale after the first booking · minor
- **Repro:**
  1. As a fresh academician at 390, make a booking through Hızlı rezervasyon ("Rezerve edildi").
  2. Open Diğer › Başlarken.
- **Observed:** "0 / 5" and "İlk rezervasyonunuzu yapın, yapılacak". The server already returns `first_booking=true`; desktop or a reload shows 1 / 5.
- **Expected:** spec §4.3: the tick shows after a refetch.
- **Where:** the booking mutations do not invalidate `welcomeKeys.state` (`frontend/src/lib/api/welcome.ts`).
- **Fix:** invalidate `["welcome","state"]` in the quick-reserve / booking mutations' `onSuccess`, or refetch the state when the checklist sheet or panel opens.

### WG10-F04 · Phone toast names a tab that does not exist ("Daha") · minor
- **Observed:** after "Bitir" on the phone, the toast says "Tur bitti. Başlarken listesi Daha sekmesinde.", but the tab bar item is "Diğer" (`glass.shell.more`). The user does not know where to tap.
- **Where:** `frontend/messages/tr.json:6554` (`welcome.tour.donePhone`); the tab label is from `glass.shell.more` = "Diğer".
- **Fix:** change the copy to "…Diğer sekmesinde." and check the EN copy against the EN tab label.

### WG10-F05 · Sign-in toast covers the welcome sheet's buttons on the phone · minor
- **Observed:** on the first sign-in at 390, the "Hoş geldiniz, Ayla" toast sits on top of the sheet's "Sonra / Turu başlat" row for its lifetime. The first screen a new person sees has its second action hidden (`shots/wg10-a03-ac-first-sheet-390.webp`).
- **Fix:** suppress the sign-in toast when `GET /welcome` returns a sheet, or offset toasts above the sheet (the sonner `offset` / `toastOptions` while `useWelcomeStore().sheet` is set).

### WG10-F06 · "Bir filtreyle salon arayın" ticks without a filter · minor
- **Observed:** opening /find-room (here through the "Salon bul" card) marks `find_room_filter` done. The item text promises a filter search.
- **Where:** `backend/app/services/welcome.py:270` (`MANUAL_ITEMS` includes `find_room_filter`; the client posts `item_done` on page view).
- **Fix:** post `item_done` from /find-room only when a filter changes, or rename the item "Boş derslik bul sayfasını açın".

### WG10-F07 · Tour scrim flashes off on the cross-route step · minor
- **Observed:** going from step 1 (nav-reserve) to step 2 (`/reserve`, reserve-when), no overlay and no card exist for 17 frames at 1440 and 21–22 frames at 390. The page shows unscrimmed for about 300 ms.
- **Expected:** WG-09 says the scrim must not flash between steps.
- **Where:** `frontend/src/components/welcome/tour/tour-runner.tsx` (route push + anchor wait).
- **Fix:** keep the overlay mounted, with the card hidden, while waiting for the next anchor.

### WG10-F08 · Sheet open drops frames at 1440 (environment caveat) · minor
- **Observed:** a rAF sampler over the sheet open (glass `blur(32px) saturate(1.8)`) found 9 of 20 frame gaps over 25 ms (max 83 ms). The tour step had 11 (max 50 ms). With reduced motion at 390, 1 gap.
- **Caveat:** this is headless software rendering on a host shared by 4 testers.
- **Fix:** re-measure in the motion-audit `welcome` block on a GPU runner. If it holds, drop the blur radius during the open animation.

### WG10-F09 · Phone checklist reads the progress twice to screen readers · minor
- **Observed:** the aria snapshot of `checklist-sheet` has `paragraph: 5 adımdan 0 tanesi tamam` twice (header and body).
- **Fix:** mark one of them `aria-hidden` or remove it.

### WG10-F10 · /reserve feature chips are 28 px tall on the phone · minor (outside WG files)
- **Observed:** `qr-feature-1` "Bilgisayar laboratuvarı" 162×28 and `qr-feature-2` 152×28 fail M-02 on /reserve at 390 (the spec §9 page list).
- **Fix:** add `pointer-coarse:min-h-11` on the quick-reserve feature chips (W3 reserve owner).

### WG10-F11 · Spec fixture password · minor
- `frontend/e2e/wg-helpers.ts:15` (`PASSWORD`) and `e2e/wg-hints.spec.ts:78` create accounts with a literal password, per the brief's rule.
