## 4. Findings

### CH12-F01 · major · The admin weekly cards say "no bookings this term" because the Güz window ends on 4 Oct
- **Status:** fixed in FIX-F: `backend/app/services/chart_stats.py` `term_weeks` = week rows ∪ 1..week_count (missing weeks dated by the term's weekly arithmetic). Test `tests/test_fix_f_2026_10_18.py::test_ch12_f01_*` (calendar cut to 2 rows, booking in week 3 counted, 15 weeks listed). Recommended option taken.
- **Repro:** `yonetici.test` → /admin › "Bu dönem": "Haftalık rezervasyonlar" and "Haftalık talep sonuçları", at 1440 and 390.
- **Observed:**
  - Both cards say "Henüz gösterilecek veri yok · Bu dönemde henüz rezervasyon yok." / "… onaya gönderilen talep yok."
  - `GET /bookings/stats/weekly?term_id=2` answers `from 2026-09-21, to 2026-10-04` with 2 weeks of zeros. Today is 18 Oct, and the term is "21 Eyl · 14 hafta" in the term switcher.
  - `personel.test` and `akademisyen.ayse` have C 606 requests on 22–23 Oct (one approved), which fall outside the window.
- **Expected:** the 14-week term with the current week hatched and the October bookings counted.
- **Where:** `smartsched/backend/app/services/chart_stats.py:101`. The week numbers come from the calendar's week rows, and only weeks 1–2 exist for Güz. This is the same calendar gap as AS20-F02 (peaks diluted the other way).
- **Fix:** take `max(week rows, term.week_count)` (extend missing weeks as lecture weeks dated from `term.start_date`). When today is after the last known week but inside `term.end_date`, never answer "no bookings"; say "takvimde N hafta tanımlı". Add a pytest with a 2-row calendar and a booking in week 5.
