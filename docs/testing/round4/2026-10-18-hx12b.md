## 2. Setup, method and the pick

- **Why these 12:**
  - The academician's and staff member's daily booking loop: /reserve, /bookings, /find-room, /my-bookings,
    /profile.
  - The approver's inbox: /approvals.
  - The planner's four landing and working screens: /dashboard, /timetable, /runs (+ /runs/[id]), /import.
  - The admin's two most visited screens: /admin/users, /settings.
  - This follows the ROADMAP HX-12 scope, with /import in place of /moderation/me and /admin. The planner opens
    /import every term; the other two are visited rarely.
- **Accounts (username):**
  - `akademisyen.ayse`: /reserve, /bookings, /find-room, /my-bookings, /profile;
  - `planlamaci.test`: /dashboard, /timetable, /runs, /runs/7, /runs/3, /import;
  - `yonetici.test`: /approvals, /admin/users, /settings.
  - `hb18.approver` sees "Bu kutuyu yalnız atanmış onaycılar görür", because no rule designates it.
- **Viewports:** 1440×900 mouse; 390×844 touch (isMobile, hasTouch); tr-TR, Europe/Istanbul. Today is Sunday 18 Oct.
- **By hand (Playwright, as a person clicks):**
  - open each page and take the first-screen screenshot;
  - open "Gelişmiş" where it exists;
  - do the main action where it is safe:
    - book through the grid and through quick reserve, then cancel (bookings 23, 25, 26, all cancelled or undone);
    - run "Ara" on /find-room;
    - open and Esc the "Yeni kullanıcı" dialog.
- **Measured:**
  - visible primaries (filled tint buttons);
  - numbers with no label;
  - English words and raw keys;
  - MOBILE M-01 (sideways scroll), M-02 (targets < 44 px), M-03 (inputs < 16 px), M-05 (primary in the bottom
    40 %, using the MOBILE.md route matrix locator);
  - `document.activeElement` after the main action.

## 4. Findings

### Major

**HX12B-F01 · /bookings at 1440 on a closed day is a blank page · major**
- Repro: sign in as `akademisyen.ayse` on Sunday and open /bookings at 1440.
- Observed: under the legend there is nothing, no grid and no sentence
  ([shot](shots/hx12b-bookings-1440.webp)). The phone says "Bu gün rezerve edebileceğiniz boş ders saati yok." but
  offers no next step. An ex-CRBS user asks "where do I click?".
- Expected: CRBS opens a bookable week, or says the day is closed and offers the next open day.
- Where: `components/bookings/bookings-view.tsx` / `booking-grid.tsx` (empty-grid branch).
- Fix: when the day has no bookable periods, show "Bugün rezervasyona kapalı." with a button
  "Pazartesi 19 Ekim'e git" on both viewports. Better, open on the next open day by default.

**HX12B-F02 · /find-room: a default date that finds nothing, and 12 controls with no Gelişmiş · major**
- Repro: as `akademisyen.ayse`, open /find-room at 1440 and press "Ara".
- Observed:
  - The result is "0 boş oda · 0/1 tarih açık", because the date defaults to today, Sunday.
  - 12 controls are visible before "Ara", which sits half below the fold at 1440
    ([shot](shots/hx12b-find-room-1440.webp)).
  - §9 rule 4 says ≤ 7 visible fields, and rule 6 says defaults that work.
- Fix:
  - Default the date to the next open teaching day.
  - At 1440 keep Ne zaman, Tarih, Saat (Başlangıç–Bitiş) and Kaç kişi. Put Sınav, Özellikler, Binalar, Arama metni,
    "Dolu derslikleri de göster" and "Bu haftanın diğer günlerine de bak" behind "Gelişmiş", as the phone already
    does with "Filtreler".
  - Replace "0/1 tarih açık" with "Seçtiğiniz gün rezervasyona kapalı."

**HX12B-F03 · /my-bookings: the only primary button resets the calendar link · major**
- Repro: as `akademisyen.ayse`, open /my-bookings at 1440.
- Observed: the one filled button on the page is "Özel bağlantıyı sıfırla" in "Takvime eşitle"
  ([shot](shots/hx12b-my-bookings-1440.webp)). Pressing it breaks existing calendar subscriptions. It is not this
  page's job, and §9 rule 1 says there is one primary per screen.
- Where: `components/bookings/calendar-sync.tsx`.
- Fix: make it a secondary or ghost button behind "Gelişmiş", with a confirm that names the effect ("Eski bağlantı
  çalışmaz olur"). Leave the page with no filled button, or with "Boş derslik bul".

**HX12B-F04 · /reserve at 1440: focus falls to `<body>` after "Rezerve et" and after "Geri al" · major**
- Repro: as `akademisyen.ayse` at 1440, open /reserve, pick room A 105 and press "Rezerve et". Then press "Geri al".
- Observed: `document.activeElement` is `<body>` after both. At 390 focus goes to the receipt title, then back to
  the room (correct).
- Where: `components/quick-reserve/quick-reserve-view.tsx:251` (`focusAfter` targets the radios), plus the
  receipt's focus effect.
- Fix: focus `qr-receipt-title` on every viewport after a booking. After "Geri al", focus the room that was booked.

**HX12B-F05 · /runs/[id]: two primaries and a wall of figures · major**
- Repro: as `planlamaci.test`, open /runs/7 at 1440 or 390.
- Observed ([shot](shots/hx12b-run7-1440.webp)):
  - "Düzenle ve yeniden çöz" (header) and "Yayınla" (hero) are both filled tint buttons.
  - The hero holds 4 bullet facts, 3 more lines ("Dersliksiz kalan…", "Sığmayan dersler…", "Profil…"), 4 KPIs
    ("Sorun 268 · Veri notları 234") and 4 bars with bare counts (547, 460, 120, 85).
  - The question the screen should answer, "Yayınlayayım mı?", gets lost. "Overwhelming".
- Fix:
  - One primary per state: "Yayınla" when the run is publishable; otherwise "Düzenle ve yeniden çöz".
  - Hero: one sentence ("524/530 ders yerleşti; 6 ders dersliksiz. Yayınlayabilirsiniz."). The four facts and bars
    go behind "Ayrıntılar".
  - Each bar gets its unit ("547 ders kapasiteye uydu").

**HX12B-F06 · Planner sees "Bu işlem için yetkiniz yok" on every run page; on phones it covers Yayınla · major**

**HX12B-F07 · /runs/3 shows "0/632 ders yerleşti" with a success tone; the list says "100/100" · major**
