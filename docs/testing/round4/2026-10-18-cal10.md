## 4. Findings

### CAL10-F01 · A department-feed holder gets 146 department cards in step 1 · MAJOR
- **Repro:**
  1. As `admin@smartsched.local`, create a role with "Bölüm ders takvimini dışa aktarma" (`calendar.department_feed`) in /admin/roles and give it to a user (`cal10.mehmet`).
  2. As that user: /profile › Takvimime ekle.
- **Observed:** "Neyi eklemek istiyorsunuz?" lists Derslerim, then one card per department alphabetically (146: "Adli Bilimler Tezli Yüksek Lisans dersleri", "Ağız ve Diş Sağlığı (iö) dersleri", …), then Rezervasyonlarım at the very bottom. There is no search. The person's own department is buried in the list. A department head has to scroll through about 150 cards to find "Rezervasyonlarım". This is the "overwhelming / where do I click?" case (`shots/cal10-t13-granted-scopes-1440.webp`).
- **Where:** `frontend/src/components/calendar-export/model.ts:49-64` (one card per department) and `steps.tsx:88-110` (`WhatStep` renders them all).
- **Fix:** show one "Bölümümün dersleri" card. When more than about 3 departments are allowed, its tap opens a searchable department picker (own and taught departments first, then "Diğer bölümler" with search). Keep the three scope cards fixed.

### CAL10-F02 · Without a panel address the subscribe buttons are shown but cannot work · MAJOR
- **Repro:**
  1. With Ayarlar › Entegrasyonlar › panel address empty (the default on this stack), sign in as `akademisyen.ayse`.
  2. Open /profile › Takvimime ekle › a scope › Abonelik bağlantısı › Onayla ve ekle.
- **Observed:** "Bağlantınız hazır" with three prominent buttons:
  - "Apple Takvim'e ekle" → `/api/v1/calendar/feeds/<tok>/…ics` (relative, not webcal://);
  - "Google Takvim'e ekle" → `cid=%2Fapi%2Fv1%2F…`;
  - "Outlook'a ekle" → `addfromweb?url=%2Fapi…`.

  None of these can subscribe. The only signal is a grey line, "Yöneticiniz panel adresini girmedi; bağlantı takvim uygulamasında çalışmayabilir." The link is shown only once, so the person has used up their link (`shots/cal10-t2-done-1440.webp`).
- **Expected:** §3.7 says "only the configured channels; never a fake button".
- **Where:**
  - `POST /calendar/subscriptions` returns relative `urls` when `public_url` is empty (`backend/app/api/v1/calendar_sync.py:328`, response `url_known:false`);
  - the done screen renders them anyway (`frontend/src/components/calendar-export/export-sheet.tsx`).
- **Fix:** when `url_known` is false, do not offer the link channel in step 2. Show "Abonelik bağlantısı için yöneticinizin panel adresini girmesi gerekiyor" and keep ".ics dosyası indir". Alternatively, derive the base from the request's forwarded host. Also add an admin warning on the KVKK switch card when the address is empty.

### CAL10-F03 · Feed ETag changes on any audit event anywhere, so 304 rarely happens · minor
- **Observed:** the same token's body is byte-identical over a minute, but the ETag changes (`92404b…` → `aeaf7d…`). `If-None-Match` with an older ETag returns 200.
- **Where:** `backend/app/api/v1/calendar_sync.py:202-204` hashes `max(AuditEvent.id)` across the whole institution, and every sign-in writes one. A client polling every 5–60 min almost never gets a 304, which defeats the CAL-05 cache.
- **Fix:** key the version on what the scope depends on: for class scopes, `max(calendar_class_revisions.last_modified)` of the term plus the scope settings; for `my_bookings`, the person's booking change stamp. Or ETag the rendered body hash.

### CAL10-F04 · Export sheet steps 2 and 3 leave focus on the sheet container · minor
- **Observed:** after "Derslerim" and after "Abonelik bağlantısı", `document.activeElement` is `DIV#cal-sheet`. The new step heading ("Nasıl eklemek istiyorsunuz?", "Takviminize şunlar gönderilecek") is not announced. Step 1 and the done screen do focus `cal-step-heading`.
- **Fix:** focus `cal-step-heading` (`tabIndex=-1`) on every step change in `export-sheet.tsx`.

### CAL10-F05 · "Bağlantıyı yenile" rotates at once without a confirmation · minor
- **Observed:** Gelişmiş › Bağlantıyı yenile immediately replaces the link. Every calendar already subscribed to the old link silently stops updating, with no warning.
- **Fix:** add a confirm: "Eski bağlantı hemen çalışmaz; takvim uygulamanızda yeni bağlantıya yeniden abone olun." The same pattern as Kaldır (`manage-list.tsx`, `cal-rotate-*`).

### CAL10-F06 · "Aydınlatma metni" link is 16 px tall on the phone · minor
- **Observed:** `a#cal-notice-link` is 99×16 at 390 on the approve step (M-02).
- **Fix:** `inline-flex min-h-11 items-center` on touch (`steps.tsx`, `cal-notice-link`).

**Spec fixture passwords (minor, per the brief):** `frontend/e2e/cal-export.spec.ts:29-30` (and `e2e/w3-hub.spec.ts:21-22`).

**Observations:**
- **Validator paste:** the subscription feed carries `SOURCE;VALUE=URI:` with its own token (RFC 7986, by design). Pasting a feed into an online validator therefore shares the token. The test link used for the validator was revoked (T14). It might be worth telling users "bağlantıyı paylaşmayın" next to Kopyala; the copy already says "Gizli tutun".
- **Other departments:** Mehmet sees no card at all for departments he may not export. The API refuses forged ids. This is simpler than §6 T13's "disabled with reason" and is accepted.
