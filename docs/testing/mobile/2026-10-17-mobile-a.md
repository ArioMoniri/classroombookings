## BLUF

**Portrait phone passes the MOBILE.md layout criteria on every staff, teacher and public route.**
- M-01, M-03, M-09 and M-11 pass everywhere, in light and dark.
- M-02 passes on every phone route except `/public` (building links 23 px tall).
- At 768, M-02 also fails on the booking grid's room headers (79×28) and the find-room "Rezerve et" buttons (110×36).
- M-04 sheets are bottom-anchored and full width, Escape closes them, and focus returns to the trigger.
- Safe-area insets are honoured in portrait.

The problems sit in four places that the round-3 testers did not measure: **layout stability, landscape, interaction cost, and 2026 platform behaviour (iOS keyboard, grabber, text scale).**

1. **CLS is poor on `/profile` and `/public/buildings/A`.**
   - `/profile`: 0.45 (phone) to 0.73 (tablet), from one 0.40 shift at about 1.15 s when the details form replaces a small `<Loading />` and the sections below jump.
   - `/public/buildings/A`: 0.77 (phone) / 0.43 (tablet). `/public/rooms/A101`: 0.11. `/bookings` on tablet: 0.28.
   - The web.dev threshold is 0.1 (good) and 0.25 (poor).
2. **Landscape is cramped.**
   - Header plus tab bar take 104 of 390 px (27 %, M-13 limit 25 %) on every app route.
   - `/reserve` leaves **32 %** of the height for content: its confirm capsule is 160 px.
   - The MOBILE.md M-13 snippet misses the tab bar, so this has never shown up as a FAIL.
3. **Opening a sheet costs 200–392 ms (INP) and 170–293 ms (LoAF), advisory.**
   - Input delay and processing are 1–15 ms, so the cost is presentation: the full-height `glass-chrome` sheet with `backdrop-filter`, painted over a 1,600-node `/bookings` page.
   - `/profile` scrolling drops 9–32 frames over 50 ms per fling (worst 433–583 ms), while the noise floor is 0–2.
4. **2026 platform gaps.**
   - On iOS (only the visual viewport resizes; Safari ignores `interactive-widget`), the book sheet's footer and note field sit under the keyboard.
   - Every bottom sheet shows a grabber, but a 500 px drag does not move or dismiss it.
   - Sheets pad with `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, the pattern Chrome's edge-to-edge guide says to avoid.
   - Body type is in `px`, so OS text scale and `<meta name="text-scale">` cannot reach it.
   - No view transitions are used.
5. **Recurring booking on the phone hides its own first step.**
   - The "Tek seferlik / Her hafta" switch sits at y 685, under 18 period chips.
   - After switching, "Önizle" is at y 928, inside the scroller, while the footer shows a disabled "0 tarihi rezerve et" (M-05 FAIL).

Ranked fixes with owners are in §4. The top 15 are summarised in the hand-off message. The hosted transitions.dev polish fix was **not spent** in phase 1; it is reserved for the book sheet in phase 2 (§5).
