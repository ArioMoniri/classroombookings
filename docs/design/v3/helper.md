## 0. BLUF

- **There is one entry point, not two:** the existing ⌘K palette grows into **"Ara veya sor"**.
  - Desktop: the top-bar trigger becomes a field-like button, "Ara veya sor… ⌘K".
  - Phones: the existing round search button next to the tab bar opens it full screen. No new tab, no floating bot.
  - Results show **navigation hits first** (pages, settings, rooms, people, courses, runs), then **"Nerede?"** answers
    from a feature index, and last the row **"Yardımcıya sor: “…”"**.
- **The helper is a second surface opened from that row (or ⌘J).** On desktop it is a **non-modal side sheet** (400 px,
  right), so the page and the spotlight stay visible. On phones it is a **full-screen sheet**.
- **"Nerede?" works without AI.** A role-filtered feature index (pages, tabs, settings, single controls, with CRBS
  synonyms) answers with a path ("Yönetim › Kullanıcılar › Davet et") and a **"Göster"** button. "Göster" navigates and
  rings the exact control, using the welcome tour's spotlight as a 1-step tour. Nothing the user cannot do is ever
  listed.
- **Generative action cards:** the model only **chooses an action from the server's registry and fills typed
  arguments**.
  - The **server builds every card** (`helper.card.v1`: `answer`, `nav`, `list`, `facts`, `form`, `confirm`,
    `result`, `limit` / `refusal` / `error`). It resolves names to ids, checks the person's own permission and
    computes the preview through the same service the page uses.
  - The client renders our components by card `type` + `action` (§4.3).
  - Writes need a click on the card's button. That sends a signed, 10-minute confirm token to
    `POST /helper/actions/confirm`, which re-checks access and the preview before it runs the page's own service.
  - Own, reversible writes take one tap: the confirm card comes prefilled. Writes that touch others show who is
    affected first. Institution-wide writes (publish, invite) are **hand-overs**: the card opens the page's own
    dialog, prefilled, and never writes itself.
  - Every result card shows what happened and "Geri al" where an undo exists.
- **The run chat stays where it is** (`/runs/[id]`, timetable edits through the guarded apply path). The helper never
  edits a timetable. It hands over with an "Çalıştırma sohbetinde aç" card. The two share the stream client, the
  composer, the banner and the card frame.
- **Memory** (architecture doc §5.4):
  - **notes** only when the user taps "Hatırla" (≤ 20, 180 days; until HB-D's remember card ships, notes are added in Profil › Yardımcı only, §17.1 #8);
  - **conversation history** for 30 days, so it continues on another device;
  - "Son işlemler" comes from the user's own confirmed actions (deferred to HB-D's recent-actions endpoint; §17.1 #6).
  - All of it is visible and deletable in Profil › Yardımcı and covered by DSR export and erasure. It is off when
    `ai_personal` consent is withdrawn.
- **Limits and status:** a status chip says **Yerel model** / **Bulut · {sağlayıcı}** / **Yapay zekâ kapalı**. The
  existing `AiUsageBanner` sits above the composer. When AI is off or over the limit, search, "Nerede?" and the
  deterministic shortcut cards still work.
- **Simplicity:** §9 is a one-page checklist for every designer, builder and tester. "Where do I click?" is a major
  finding.

## 2. Placement

### 2.1 Decision

| Option | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A. A floating chat bubble (bottom right) | **No** | G1 (only chrome floats). It hides content on phones, it is a second "search", and ex-CRBS users ignore it (Intercom-style bubbles read as marketing) |
| B. A separate search bar **and** a bot button | **No** | Two places to ask the same thing. The owner's rule: one primary action, no extra decision |
| C. **One field "Ara veya sor"** (palette) **+ a helper sheet opened from it** | **Yes** | Notion, ClickUp and Raycast pattern (research §1.3, M1, M2). Search answers 80 % of "where is it" with no AI. The sheet opens only when the user asks |
| D. Merge the run chat into the helper | **Not now** | The run chat edits timetables through `chat/apply`, `publish_guard` and `move_guard`. Merging would put integrity-guarded edits behind a generic assistant. They share components instead (§2.6). Revisit after HX-13 |

### 2.3 "Ara veya sor" results (the extended palette)

- The placeholder reads **"Sayfa, ayar, salon, kişi ya da ders arayın… veya bir soru sorun"**.
- With an **empty query**, the palette shows three groups:
  1. **Son işlemler**: up to 3 of the user's own confirmed helper actions ("A 204 rezerve edildi · dün"), each
     opening the object. *Deferred (§17.1 #6):* shown once HB-D's recent-actions endpoint lands; until then the empty
     palette shows groups 2 and 3 only.
  2. **Şunları sorabilirsiniz**: 3 starters for the user's role (§6.2).
  3. **Git**: today's list.
- With a **query**, groups appear in this fixed order, and groups without hits are hidden:

| # | Group | Source | Row | Enter does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sayfalar ve ayarlar** | Feature index (§3), role-filtered | Icon · title · grey path "Yönetim › Kullanıcılar" | Navigate |
| 2 | **Nerede?** (only when an index entry is a control *inside* a page) | Feature index `kind: control` | "Davet et düğmesi" · path · a small "Göster" hint | Navigate + spotlight (§3.3) |
| 3 | Salonlar · Kişiler · Dersler · Programlar · Çalıştırmalar | Existing palette search (role-aware as today) | As today | As today |
| 4 | **Kısayollar** | Deterministic intents (§4.5) | "Yarın 10:00 için salon ayır…" | Opens the helper with that card, no AI call |
| 5 | **Yardımcıya sor** | Always last; **first** when the query reads as a question (ends with "?", or starts with nasıl / nerede / neden / kim / ne zaman / how / where / why / can I, or has ≥ 5 words) | "Yardımcıya sor: “{query}”", with the status chip on the right | Opens the helper sheet and sends the query |

- **Matching:** the existing Turkish-aware `fold()` (İ/ı, diacritics, spaces), plus synonyms and CRBS words (§3.2),
  plus typo tolerance of one edit for words of 5 or more letters. No AI is used for ranking.
- **No hits:** "“{q}” için sonuç yok." The ask row is still offered. If AI is off: "Yardımcı şu an kapalı. Yazımı
  kontrol edin ya da Yardım › Turu yeniden başlat'ı deneyin."
- **Size:** at most 6 rows per group and 24 rows in total. The footer stays as today.

### 2.4 Helper sheet: desktop (`helper-sheet-1440.svg`)

- **Non-modal side sheet**, 400 px wide (360 at 1024–1279), docked right under the top bar, full height minus 16 px
  margins, `--radius-lg`. Glass regular is one of the ≤ 3 blur surfaces, and opaque under reduced transparency. There
  is **no scrim**: the page stays operable and visible (needed for "Göster").
- The page content does **not** reflow (no layout shift). The sheet overlays the right 400 px. On `/timetable` and the
  run view, where the right edge holds content, the sheet opens over the canvas and the canvas keeps its scroll.
- **Header:**
  - "Yardımcı" (`type-headline`);
  - the status chip (§5.2);
  - a "⋯" menu: "Yeni konuşma", "Yardımcı neleri hatırlıyor?" (→ Profil › Yardımcı), "Yalnızca metin" toggle, "Kısayollar";
  - "✕".
- **Body:** a message log. User bubbles are right-aligned as in the run chat. Assistant text is plain. **Cards** are
  full-width (§4).
- **Footer:**
  - `AiUsageBanner` (existing);
  - a context chip **"Bu sayfa: {sayfa adı}"** (removable; it sends the route and the selected entity, never page
    text). *Deferred (§17.1 #7):* added when HB-D's `context` field lands on `POST /helper/turn`; until then "bu oda"
    style requests resolve through a `choice` or a `form`;
  - `PromptComposer` (existing, 16 px), with starters on an empty thread;
  - `AiNotice` (existing).
- **Esc** closes the sheet when focus is inside it. Focus returns to the opener (palette row → the top-bar trigger).
  **F6** cycles page ↔ sheet.

### 2.5 Helper sheet: phone (`helper-390.svg` B, C)

- A **full-screen sheet** (`role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`) that slides up, with a 44 px "Kapat" (✕) at the top
  left, the title "Yardımcı", the status chip, and "⋯".
- The composer is docked at the bottom above the safe area, with 16 px text and a 44 px send button. Starters are
  44 px chips that wrap. The tab bar is hidden while the sheet is open.
- Cards are full-width. Buttons are 48 px (primary) and 44 px (secondary). Mini forms use native inputs (date, time,
  select) with 16 px text.
- **"Göster" on a phone** closes the sheet, navigates, and shows the spotlight with a **docked step card** (as in
  WG-05 on phones). The step card has a "Yardımcıya dön" button that reopens the sheet with the thread intact.
- In landscape, the sheet is `100dvh`, and the composer stays visible above the keyboard (`keyboard-clearance.tsx`).

## 3. "Nerede?" answers

### 3.1 Feature index (server `app/helper/features.yaml`, HB-01; client link map, HX-01)

- **Index source:** the architecture doc's `features.yaml` (§5.5). It holds the id, route, TR/EN title, synonyms,
  steps, the `data-tour` anchor, the permission (any-of), audiences, `actions[]` and `since`. It is searched with
  Turkish-folded BM25 by `GET /helper/search` with **no AI**, permission-filtered **before** ranking, with p95 < 50 ms
  (HB-02).
- **The client never takes a URL from the server.** It maps `feature_id` → `href` + `anchor` through
  `components/helper/feature-links.ts` (HX-01), generated from `nav-config.ts`, `ADMIN_SECTION_NAV_ITEMS`, the
  settings and profile tabs, and §3.4.
- **UX additions to the YAML** (asks to HB-01, which owns the file):
  - a `kind` field: `page` | `tab` | `setting` | `control`. It decides between the "Sayfalar ve ayarlar" and
    "Nerede?" groups;
  - a `path` for the grey path line, sent on the wire as plain text arrays `{tr[], en[]}` (and `steps` the same way;
    §17.1 #1), so the client does not keep its own copy of the index;
  - **the CRBS synonyms of §3.2**, all of them;
  - ≤ 3 steps per entry (the architecture doc allows 5; the simplicity rule wants 3).
- **Role filter:** done on the server. The client also hides any hit whose `feature_id` it cannot map, or whose
  `can(permission)` is false with `usePermissions()` (double filter). A user never sees a page they cannot open.
- **Size:** about 140 entries at launch (40 pages / tabs, 60 settings, 40 controls). Two tests guard it:
  - HB-01's fixture: ≥ 120 persona queries, top-1 ≥ 85 %;
  - HX-01's vitest: every `feature_id` → an existing route, and every anchor in `welcome-guide.md` §13 or §3.4 here.

### 3.3 Answer shapes

- **In the palette** (no AI): a row with the title and path. Enter = navigate. **Shift+Enter or the "Göster" hint =
  navigate + spotlight.**
- **In the helper** (with or without AI): the **`where` card** = wire type **`nav`** (`helper-where-1440.svg`):
  - Title: "Kullanıcı davet et".
  - Path line: "Yönetim › Kullanıcılar › **Davet et**".
  - Optional ≤ 3 numbered steps ("Adımları göster", collapsed by default).
  - Buttons: **"Göster"** (primary) and "Sayfayı aç" (secondary). A third button, "Burada yap", appears when the
    entry has a shortcut card (e.g. the `invite_user` mini form).
- **"Göster":**
  1. `router.push(href)`;
  2. wait for `[data-tour=anchor]` (≤ 3 s, as WG-05);
  3. `scrollIntoView({block:"center"})`;
  4. show the **spotlight ring + one step card** ("Davet et: Yeni kişileri e-posta ile buradan davet edersiniz.").
  - Desktop: the step card has "Anladım" and "Yardımcıya dön". **No scrim** (§17.1 #5): the 3 px `--focus` ring alone
    marks the control (5.6:1 light, 6.9:1 dark; `Highlight` in forced colours), the helper sheet stays visible, and the
    target stays **interactive**. When the target lies under the docked sheet, the sheet steps aside for the step
    (as on a phone) and "Yardımcıya dön" brings it back (audit 2026-10-18 M1).
  - Phone: the step card is docked, as in WG-05.
- **Missing anchor** (a feature flag hid it, or the page is empty): navigate only, then "Bu düğme şu an bu sayfada
  görünmüyor." plus the steps.
- **Not allowed:** the entry does not exist for this user. If the model is asked, the server answers with a `refusal`
  card: "Bu işlem hesabınızda yok. Yapabildikleriniz: …", plus the user's own nearest entries as `nav` cards. It never
  says who holds the permission, and it never links the page.
- **Hallucinated menus are impossible:** the architecture doc §5.6 (5) replaces any answer that names a page without a
  `nav` card by the index answer. The UI shows paths only from `nav` cards, never from `answer` text.

## 4. Generative action cards

### 4.1 Rules (all cards)

1. **Server-built cards only** (architecture doc §5.3).
   - The model chooses an **action** from the person's tool list (≤ 10 tools; only actions the person holds and the
     admin enabled) and fills typed `args`.
   - The server validates them (`extra="forbid"`), **resolves names to ids**, runs the read or the preview through
     the **same service as the page's REST route**, and emits a `card` SSE event built by `cards.py`.
   - The model's own words only ever appear in an `answer` card (plain text, ≤ 1200 characters, no links, no
     Markdown).
   - **The model never supplies HTML, JS, CSS, URLs, API paths, ids or button labels.**
2. **Typed contract** `helper.card.v1` (pydantic on the server, published at `GET /helper/schema`; zod generated from
   it on the client, with snapshot tests on both sides).
   - The client chooses the **presentation** from `type` + `action` (§4.3), so `confirm` for `create_booking`
     renders the room / day / period layout.
   - An unknown `type` or `action` renders as an `answer` with a `nav` fallback. Nothing is ever rendered from
     untyped data.
3. **The site's own features only.**
   - Writes go through `POST /helper/actions/confirm` with the card's token. The server re-checks access, re-runs the
     preview and compares its hash, then calls the page's service with the person's `Access`.
   - `booking_policies`, the approval mode, rate limits and audit (`helper.action.<name>`, `via="helper"`) all apply.
     The registry's drift test (HB-03) proves each action matches its REST route.
   - **Hand-overs** (`publish`, `invite`, calendar) open the page's own dialog, prefilled from the card. The helper
     itself never calls those endpoints in v1.
4. **Confirm by risk class:**
   - **R0 (read / navigate):** `nav`, `list`, `facts`. No confirm.
   - **R1 (own, reversible write):** `create_booking` (own), `cancel_my_booking`, `withdraw_my_request`.
     - When every required argument resolves, the server sends the **`confirm` card directly** (no `form` step).
     - The card *is* the confirmation: one primary button names the effect ("Rezerve et: A 204 · yarın
       10:00–10:50").
     - "Değiştir" turns it into the `form`. Changing a field re-runs the preview (debounced 300 ms) and gets a new
       token.
   - **R2 (affects other people):** `decide_approval`, and cancelling someone else's booking with
     `cancel_other_booking`.
     - The preview always names who is affected ("P. T.'ye bildirim gider").
     - Reject needs a reason, as on the page. One click, then it executes.
   - **R3 (institution-wide):** publish, invite, settings. **Hand-over only:** a `facts` card with the server preview
     and a button that opens the page's own dialog (two deliberate steps). The helper never publishes or invites by
     itself.
   - **Only an `isTrusted` pointer or keyboard activation of the card's button confirms.** Typed "evet" / "yes" in the
     composer is answered with "Onaylamak için karttaki düğmeye basın." and confirms nothing.
5. **Freshness:** the token expires after 10 minutes. On confirm, a changed preview answers 409 with a new preview.
   The card then shows "Bu öneri eskidi" or the new preview with "Tekrar onayla". A taken slot gives alternatives
   ("Başka saat öner").
6. **Result and undo:** a `result` card follows: a check, one line, "Aç" (via `feature_id`), and **"Geri al"** when it
   carries an `undo_token` (24 h or until the booking starts, `POST /helper/actions/undo`). A sonner toast with "Geri
   al" shows for 8 s, as in the run chat. Errors show the server's `message_tr` / `message_en` inside the card, and
   the inputs stay filled.
7. **One card in focus.** A new card collapses older *pending* `form` / `confirm` cards to a single line
   ("Rezervasyon önerisi · bekliyor"). At most one pending R2 card exists per thread.
8. **Switches:** the admin enables actions per audience (`ai.helper.actions`, HB-15). Users can choose **"Yalnızca
   metin"**, which turns `list` / `facts` into short text plus `nav` cards. `confirm` cards stay, because they are the
   only way to act.
9. **Taint:** when a turn has read other people's free text (booking notes, request comments), the server drops write
   tools for that turn (architecture doc §2.4). The UI then offers `nav` to the page instead of a `confirm` card,
   with the line "Bu işlemi sayfadan yapın."

### 4.2 Wire types → presentations

| Wire `type` (architecture doc §5.3) | UX name | Presentation rules |
|---|---|---|
| `answer` | text | Plain paragraph(s) in the log, no bubble; `source: index` shows a small "Dizinden" caption |
| `nav` | **where** | Path line from the client's link map, ≤ 3 steps (collapsed), "Göster" (when an `anchor` exists) / "Sayfayı aç" / "Burada yap" (when the entry has an action) |
| `list` | e.g. find_room, my_bookings, approvals_list | ≤ 5 rows visible (the wire allows 20) + "Tümünü {sayfa}'da aç"; row actions are 44 px text buttons; columns collapse to two lines on phones |

### 4.5 Deterministic shortcuts (work with AI off)

A small client parser (`components/helper/intents.ts`) reads common phrasings:
- dates: bugün, yarın, perşembe, 12 Kasım;
- periods and times: 3. saat, 10:00, "öğleden sonra";
- room codes: A 204;
- headcounts: "12 kişilik".

It maps them to an action with **partial arguments**: "yarın 10 a 204" → `create_booking`; "boş salon 12 kişi" →
`find_free_rooms`; "takvim" → the calendar `nav`; "davet" → the invite hand-over.
- They show in the palette group **Kısayollar**.
- Selecting one asks the server for the card **without a model call** (`POST /helper/actions/{name}/card` with the
  partial `args`, which returns `confirm`, `form` or `list`; a UX ask to HB-05 / HB-07, §17).
- The same confirm rules apply.
- This also gives keyboard users the CRBS path (week → free slot → book) in one line, with AI off or at the limit.

## 8. Accessibility

- **Palette:** cmdk combobox semantics (as today). The ask row's accessible name is "Yardımcıya sor: {q}, yapay zekâ,
  {durum}". The "Nerede?" row name includes the path ("Davet et, Yönetim, Kullanıcılar"). Shift+Enter = Göster, and
  it is listed in the footer hints. Group headings are announced.
- **Desktop sheet:** `role="complementary"` + `aria-label="Yardımcı"`, **non-modal** (no focus trap). On open, focus
  goes to the composer. **Esc** closes from inside the sheet. **F6** cycles landmarks. On close, focus returns to the
  opener, or to the page's `h1` when the opener is gone.
- **Phone sheet:** `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, focus trap (`components/ui/focus-trap.ts`), the title as
  the label.
- **Message log:** `role="log"`, `aria-live="polite"`, `aria-busy` while streaming. The finished reply is announced
  once, not per token (as the run chat). "Düşünüyor…" is `role="status"`.
- **Cards:** each card is a `region` (`role="group"`, `aria-labelledby` = card title). Fields have visible labels and
  `aria-describedby` for errors. The **primary button's name states the effect and the object**. A disabled R2/R3
  button has `aria-describedby` → the reason ("Önce etkilenen kişileri görün", "Engel: …").
  - After confirm, focus moves to the **result line** (`tabIndex=-1`) and a polite live message repeats it. After
    undo, focus goes to "Geri alındı." Focus never falls to `<body>` (COMMON rule 6).
- **Spotlight:** as WG-05. The target gets `aria-describedby` → the step text. Esc ends it, and focus stays on the
  target.
- **Status chip:** a button with `aria-haspopup="dialog"`. The word carries the state.
- **Touch and zoom:** 44 px targets, 48 px primary on phones, 16 px inputs, nothing hover-only. It reflows at 320 px
  and 200 % zoom (cards stack, the sheet becomes full screen under 640 px).
- **Contrast:** AA in light and dark; forced colours: the chip dot gets an outline, and the card border uses
  `CanvasText`.
- **Language:** answers follow the UI locale. The `lang` attribute is set per message when the model answers in the
  other language (EN in a TR UI).

## 9. Simplicity rules for the whole app (checklist for designers, builders and testers)

Use this list on every screen review. A "no" is a **major** finding with a screenshot (COMMON.md Simplicity principle).

1. **One primary action** per screen, named with a plain Turkish verb + object ("Rezerve et", "Davet gönder"). Other
   actions are secondary or in "⋯".
2. **"Where do I click?" in 5 seconds.** A first-time ex-CRBS user finds the primary action without scrolling at 1440
   and at 390.
3. **The CRBS path stays two taps:** week view → free slot → book. No wizard, AI or dialog may be inserted into it.
4. **Advanced behind "Gelişmiş"** (or a sheet). Never more than 7 visible fields in a form, and never more than 5
   filters visible on a phone.
5. **No unexplained numbers.** Every number has a label and a unit, and a chart has a sentence that says what it
   means. No KPI wall on an academician's screen.
6. **Defaults that work.** A new institution can book on day one without opening Settings. Every setting has a sane
   default and "Varsayılana dön".
7. **Words people already use.** CRBS and university words (salon, derslik, ders saati, dönem, tatil, bölüm). No
   internal terms (run, solver, constraint, enum keys) on non-planner screens.
8. **Every feature can be found by search.** If it is not in the feature index (§3.1) with its synonyms, the feature
   is not done.
9. **AI is optional.** Every task the helper can do has a normal page path. With AI off, nothing on the page breaks
   and no empty AI box is shown.
10. **Writes are explicit.** A button names its effect. Anything that touches other people shows who. Institution-wide
    changes always show a preview first. Undo is offered where possible.
11. **One interruption at a time.** At most one sheet, hint or banner competes for attention (welcome guide §3 "one
    interruption per session").
12. **Calm motion.** Motion only says what changed, where it went or whether it worked; ≤ 300 ms; reduced motion
    respected.
13. **Phones are first-class.** 44 px targets, 16 px inputs, no hover-only affordances, the primary action within
    thumb reach.
14. **Empty, loading and error states** say what happened and what to do next, in one sentence each.
