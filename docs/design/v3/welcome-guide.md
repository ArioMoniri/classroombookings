## 0. BLUF

- There are **four parts**, all role-aware. Each one can be switched off by the institution.
  1. **Welcome sheet** on the first sign-in: a hero, a one-line promise and 3–5 cards about the parts *this person can
     use*, with "Kısa turu başlat" and "Sonra".
  2. **Guided tour** (optional, 4–7 steps) over the real screens of that role. It is never auto-started.
  3. **"Başlarken" checklist** (3–6 items) that ticks itself from real events (a booking exists, a channel is linked).
     It lives in the sidebar footer on desktop and on the first row of "Daha" on phones.
  4. **Feature hints**, once per release, for the wave-3 features: quick reserve, calendar export, admin AI, ops and
     integrations.
- **Help › "Turu yeniden başlat"** (user menu and ⌘K) reopens the sheet and the tour at any time.
- **Audiences come from permissions** (and the instructor link), computed by the server: academician, staff,
  approver, department admin, viewer, planner and admin. Kiosk/public pages never show a guide.
- **State is server-side per user** (`welcome_progress`), so it is the same on every device. At most **one automatic
  interruption per session**.
- **Admins** switch each part on or off, edit the texts per audience (TR/EN, versioned, optional "show again"), and see
  completion rates for groups of 5 or more people, with no per-person list.
- **Motion:** 9 free transitions.dev recipes, re-implemented on our springs (≤ 300 ms). There is one Lottie hero per
  audience, played once to its poster frame. Reduced motion gives instant states, ≤ 100 ms fades and the poster PNG.
- **Library:** React Joyride 3.2 (MIT), lazy-loaded, controlled, with our own step card. driver.js 1.10 (MIT) is the
  fallback.

## 3. Interaction patterns (named)

| Pattern | Where | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Role-aware welcome sheet** | First eligible page after sign-in | Dialog (desktop) / bottom sheet (phone). It shows the cards of the primary audience plus one "Ayrıca" card per secondary audience, **max 5** |
| **Offer, don't force** | Sheet, hints | A tour starts only from a button. Nothing auto-advances |
| **Spotlight + step card** | Tour | A dim scrim with a hole around one real control, one moving focus ring, and a step card anchored to the target (desktop) or docked as a sheet (phone) |
| **Self-ticking checklist** | Sidebar footer pill, "Daha" first row, home card | Items tick from server signals. "Bitti olarak işaretle" exists only for view-only items |
| **One interruption per session** | Global | The sheet **or** one hint is auto-shown per session, never both, and never over another dialog |
| **Inline hint on phones** | Feature hints | On phones, a hint is a card at the top of the page, not a popover |
| **Restart from Help** | User menu › Yardım, ⌘K | "Turu yeniden başlat" reopens the sheet for the current audiences |
| **Delta welcome** | Role change | Only the new audiences' cards, titled "Yeni yetkileriniz var" |

## 4. The parts

### 4.1 Welcome sheet (`welcome-sheet-1440.svg`, `welcome-sheet-390.svg`)

- **Desktop:** a centred dialog, 640 px wide, `--radius-lg`, glass regular material on the `--overlay` scrim.
  - The hero is 150 px high (§8.2).
  - The title is "SmartSched'e hoş geldiniz, {ad}" (`type-large-title`), with one lead line and a badge naming the
    primary audience and the department ("Öğretim elemanı · Psikoloji").
  - The cards sit in a 2-column grid, 116 px each.
  - Footer: the primary "Kısa turu başlat (1 dk)", the secondary "Sonra", and the link "Başlarken listesi". Below it,
    a hint line: "Bu rehberi her zaman Yardım › Turu yeniden başlat ile açabilirsiniz."
  - A tertiary link "Bir daha gösterme" sits in the footer, right-aligned after "Başlarken listesi" on ≥ 1024 px and
    under the buttons on phones.
- **Phone:** a bottom sheet with one large detent (top at about 25 % of the viewport) and a grabber.
  - It shows one card per page with step dots ("1 / 4") and the buttons "İleri" (primary, 50 px), "Sonra" and
    "Turu başlat" (44 px). On the last page, "İleri" becomes "Başlayalım".
  - Swiping pages is optional. The buttons and dots carry the same state.
- Cards are **informational buttons**: activating a card closes the sheet (state `completed`) and opens that part.
  "Hızlı rezervasyon" opens `/reserve`. On the last page or after "Başlayalım", focus goes to the page's `h1`.
- **Closing:**
  - "Sonra", ✕, Esc or dragging the sheet down snoozes it (`snoozed`, shown again at the first sign-in 3 days or more
    later, at most twice).
  - "Bir daha gösterme" sets `dismissed`.
  - "Kısa turu başlat" sets `completed` and starts the tour.
  - After any of these, focus returns to the element that had focus before the sheet opened, or else to the page's
    `h1`.

### 4.2 Guided tour (`welcome-tour-1440.svg`, `welcome-tour-390.svg`)

- 4–7 steps (§5), anchored to `data-tour="<anchor>"` attributes on real controls. Each step has a title (≤ 4 words),
  a body (≤ 2 sentences), "Adım n / N", dots, "Geri", "İleri" / "Bitir" and an always-visible "Turu bitir".
- **Engine:** React Joyride 3.2 in controlled mode (`stepIndex`), with `tooltipComponent` = our `TourCard`, its own
  animations and beacons off, and `locale` from `welcome.tour.*` keys. A step may carry a `route`. The engine calls
  `router.push(route)`, waits for the anchor (`MutationObserver`, 3 s), then shows the step. A missing anchor skips
  the step and logs a client warning.
- **Spotlight:** one scrim layer (`--overlay`, no backdrop-filter) with a hole, plus **one** ring element (3 px
  `--focus`, radius = target radius + 4 px) that moves between targets. The target stays operable only on steps
  marked `interactive` (e.g. "Bir saat seçin"). Otherwise clicks on the hole advance nothing and do nothing.
- **Escape** = "Turu bitir": state `dismissed_at_step`, and a toast "Tur kapatıldı. Yardım › Turu yeniden başlat ile
  dönebilirsiniz." Focus returns to the step's target if it is focusable, otherwise to the page's `h1`.
- **Last step:** "Bitir" sets state `completed`. The toast reads "Tur bitti. Başlarken listesi kenar çubuğunda." (on
  phones: "…Daha sekmesinde.").
- While a tour runs, the global shortcuts (`use-global-shortcuts.ts`) are paused and ⌘K is disabled. They come back
  when the tour ends.

### 4.3 "Başlarken" checklist (`welcome-checklist-1440.svg`, `welcome-checklist-390.svg`)

- **Desktop:** a sidebar-footer pill "Başlarken 2 / 5" with a bar and "Sıradaki: …". It is a button that opens a
  420 px popover panel with the title, "5 adımdan 2'si tamam", the bar, rows and "Gizle". On the home page of the
  audience (`/dashboard`, `/reserve` or `/admin`), the same list is also a card until it is complete or hidden.
- **Phone:** the first row of the "Daha" tab, opening a full-screen list. There is no floating launcher (G1).
- **Rows:** a check circle, a title, a one-line subtitle and a chevron. Done rows are muted, stay listed and are not
  struck through. Activating a row navigates to the place where the item is done, and it may start a 1–2 step
  mini-tour for that item.
- **Ticking:** the server computes `done` for detectable items on `GET /welcome` (§6.4). For view-only items, the
  client posts `item_done` when the page is opened.
- **When all are done:** a success check (§8) and "Hepsi tamam. Listeyi gizleyebilirsiniz." The pill hides itself
  after the next sign-in.
- "Gizle" hides the list (state `hidden`). It returns via Yardım › Başlarken listesi.

### 4.4 Help menu and ⌘K (`welcome-hints-help.svg` C)

User menu › **Yardım** group: "Turu yeniden başlat", "Başlarken listesi" (with "2 / 5"), "Neler yeni" (the existing
sheet, with a dot when unread) and "Klavye kısayolları" (the existing sheet; hidden on touch as today). ⌘K gets
the commands "Turu yeniden başlat" and "Başlarken listesi". On phones the group is a row group in "Daha".

- **Rules:**
  - A hint shows only to people who can use the feature.
  - It shows only once per user, as state `seen`.
  - It shows only to accounts created before the feature's release (new accounts meet the feature in the sheet and
    tour instead).
  - It expires 60 days after release.
  - At most one hint auto-shows per page view, and only when no sheet, tour or dialog is open.
  - Hints on the feature's own page are inline strips (non-modal, no focus move). A hint on a nav entry is a small
    non-modal popover (`role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="false"`) that keeps focus where it is.
  - **Buttons:** "Dene" (go there or start the 1–2 step mini-tour) and "Anladım". Esc or an outside click count as
    "Anladım".
  - Every hint ends with a quiet link "Tüm yenilikler" → "Neler yeni".
- **Registry:** the server holds the list (key, audiences, release version / date, route, anchor, expiry). The copy
  lives in `messages/{tr,en}.json` `welcome.hints.*`, and an admin may override it (§7).

### 4.6 Admin page (`welcome-admin-1440.svg`, `welcome-admin-390.svg`)

This is a new setup section, **Yönetim › Karşılama rehberi** (`/admin/welcome`, permission `setup.settings` or
`planning.admin`), with three tabs.

**Genel** has six switches:
1. Karşılama rehberi
2. Rehberli tur
3. Başlarken listesi
4. Yeni özellik ipuçları
5. Herkese ipucu göster (off by default; shows hints even to people who closed earlier ones)
6. Önce kendinizde deneyin (admin preview: opens the sheet for a chosen audience without writing progress)

**Metinler** has one chip per audience. For each, the admin edits the title, lead line and each card's title and
body, in Turkish and English side by side.
- Editable: text only. Order, links and icons are fixed by the product, so a text cannot point at a page the role
  cannot open.
- Placeholders `{ad}` / `{name}` and `{bölüm}` / `{department}` only.
- 80 / 200 character limits.
- "Varsayılana dön" per field, "Önizle" (renders the sheet in place), "Kaydet".
- On save, the admin is asked: "Kaydedince bu kitleye rehber yeniden gösterilsin mi? ☐ Evet, 'Güncellendi'
  etiketiyle". "Yes" bumps that audience's `content_version`.
- Each save shows "Son değişiklik: {ad}, {zaman}". A stale save (version conflict) answers 409: "Bu metni siz
  düzenlerken {ad} değiştirdi. Sayfayı yenileyip yeniden deneyin."

**Tamamlanma** shows the last 7 / 30 / 90 days per audience: Gördü (saw the sheet), Tur (finished the tour, %),
Liste ≥ %80, Kapattı (dismissed, %), and the median days to the first booking (booking audiences only).
- Groups under 5 people show "5'ten az", with no numbers.
- There is no per-person list and no export.
- The numbers are computed from `welcome_progress` (aggregate SQL) and never sent anywhere.

## 6. When it shows, and storage

### 6.1 Triggers (the server decides; the client only renders `show`)

| # | Trigger | Condition | Shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | First sign-in | No `welcome` row, guide enabled, eligible route (§6.2) | Sheet (`state=shown`) |
| T2 | Snoozed | `snoozed_until` < now and `snooze_count` ≤ 2 | Sheet again |
| T3 | Role change | The audience set's hash ≠ the stored `audience_hash` and a new audience was added | Delta sheet "Yeni yetkileriniz var" with only the new audiences' cards. A lost audience shows nothing, and its checklist items disappear |
| T4 | Admin "show again" | That audience's `content_version` > the stored one | Sheet with a "Güncellendi" badge |
| T5 | Feature release | A hint is eligible (§4.5) and no sheet is due this session | One hint |
| T6 | User asks | Help › Turu yeniden başlat, ⌘K, checklist row | Sheet (or tour/mini-tour). Not counted against the session budget |

At most one of T1–T5 per session (`sessionStorage["ss.welcome.auto"]`; the server also returns a single `show`).

### 6.2 Never auto-show

- `/setup`, `/login`, `/welcome` (the SSO first-sign-in page), `/reset-password`, `/public/**`, the slot display,
  `?kiosk=1`, `?embed=1` and print.
- A first route that is not the audience's home: a deep link from an e-mail or notification (`/approvals/123`) is
  never interrupted. The sheet waits for the next visit to home.
- Maintenance mode for users who are not exempt.
- When another dialog or sheet is open, or while the tour runs.
- When the institution switched the part off.

### 6.3 Data model (WG-01; migration **next head**, today `0040_welcome_guide` with `down_revision = "0039_calendar_classes"` unless another lane lands a later head first)

```
welcome_progress
  id PK · user_id FK users ON DELETE CASCADE · guide_key varchar(64)   -- "sheet", "tour", "checklist", "hint.quick_reserve", …
  audience varchar(32) · audience_hash char(16) · content_version int
  state varchar(16)          -- shown | snoozed | dismissed | started | completed | hidden | seen
  step int null · items json (done item keys + done_at) · snooze_count int · snoozed_until timestamptz null
  first_shown_at · updated_at · completed_at null
  UNIQUE (user_id, guide_key)
welcome_content
  id PK · audience varchar(32) · locale varchar(5) · field varchar(64) · text varchar(200)
  version int · updated_by_id FK users · updated_at
  UNIQUE (audience, locale, field)
settings group "welcome" (existing Setting rows): enabled, sheet, tour, checklist, hints, hints_for_all,
  content_version.<audience>, min_cohort (5)
```

Both tables work on SQLite and PostgreSQL. RLS follows W3-SEC's per-user plan, so a user reads only their own
progress rows. The existing `user.{id}.*` settings rows stay for the changelog. They are not reused here, because rates
and per-step state need columns.

**Checklist signals (server):**
- `profile`: department and language set.
- `first_booking`: the user has a booking.
- `channel`: a linked channel or an explicit e-mail preference.
- `calendar`: a calendar subscription or connection (CAL-03 tables). Before CAL lands, `mine.ics` token use counts.
- `first_decision`: an approval decided by the user.
- `book_for_other`: a booking whose owner is not its creator.
- `recurring`: a series created by the user.
- `intake_done`: a committed intake batch.
- `first_run`: a run created by the user.
- `published`: a run published by the user.
- `org_ready`: org name and time zone set.
- `rooms_added`: at least 1 room.
- `users_invited`: at least 1 other user or an open invite.
- `mail_ready`: `smtp_configured()` or a mail provider connected.
- View-only items (`requests_seen`, `timetable_seen`, `compare_seen`, `welcome_reviewed`, `find_room_filter`) use
  `item_done` from the client.

### 6.5 KVKK

- **Purpose:** show or hide the guide. The data is minimal: no content, no clicks, no time on page.
- The rows are deleted with the account (cascade), covered by DSR export and erasure (`services/kvkk.py`, a small
  additive change, asked of its owner), and listed in the processing inventory.
- Stats are aggregate only, with k ≥ 5, no drill-down and no export.
- No third-party script and no cross-border transfer.

### 8.3 Glass and performance

- **Backdrop-filter surfaces during the welcome:** the shell bar plus the sheet (2 of 3). The scrim has no
  backdrop-filter.
- The tour card and hint popover are **opaque** (`--surface-raised`). They float over a scrim, so they do not need glass,
  and they would add a third and fourth blur.
- The ring and scrim are `pointer-events:none` (except the scrim's click-catcher on non-interactive steps) and
  `aria-hidden`.
- Only transform and opacity animate. The scrim hole is re-rendered per step and never tweened (`clip-path` animation
  is banned).
- `will-change` is applied only while dragging the phone sheet.
- **Audit:** add the welcome moments to `e2e/motion-audit.spec.ts` (≤ 3 dropped frames per interaction, nothing over
  300 ms except the Lottie, reduced motion = end state).

### 8.5 As built (WG-09, 2026-10-18; ledger wg-09-2026-10-18)

The owner's rule for this surface is calm and short: every one-shot UI motion ends within **240 ms, delays included**,
and nothing moves under reduced motion. The shared springs settle in 270–285 ms, so the welcome surfaces use tweens
from `components/welcome/motion.ts` (`welcomeMotion`, built from `dur`/`ease`/`staggerRule` tokens). Nobody grabs or
re-targets these elements with a finger, so tweens lose nothing here. motion still starts every new tween from the
current value. The phone sheet's drag stays on ui/dialog. `motion.test.ts` checks the ceiling, and it also fails if a
welcome component uses `springs.*`, a raw duration, or animates without reading the reduced-motion setting.

## 12. Accessibility

- **Sheet:** `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, `aria-labelledby` = the title and `aria-describedby` = the lead.
  - Initial focus goes to the title (`tabIndex=-1`), so screen readers hear the greeting first. Tab moves through the
    cards and buttons.
  - Esc = Sonra. Focus is trapped (`components/ui/focus-trap.ts` behaviour) and returns to the opener or the `h1`.
  - On phones, the pages are a `group` with `aria-roledescription="slayt"` and `aria-label="1 / 4"`. The dots are
    `aria-hidden` (the counter text carries the state).
- **Tour:** the step card has `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, title and body references (Joyride's own ARIA),
  and our `aria-live="polite"` region announcing "Adım 2 / 5: Taleplerim".
  - The target gets `aria-describedby` → the step body while the step is active.
  - Keys: Tab is trapped in the card; Enter = İleri; ← / → = Geri / İleri (only while the card has focus); Esc =
    Turu bitir.
  - Interactive steps (`interactive: true`) let Tab reach the target and come back.
  - Screen-reader users get the same steps; the visual ring is `aria-hidden`.
- **Checklist:**
  - The pill is a `button` with `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` and the label "Başlarken, 5 adımdan 2'si tamam".
  - Rows are links with "tamamlandı" / "yapılacak" in the accessible name.
  - The progress bar is `role="progressbar"` with `aria-valuenow` / `aria-valuemax`.
  - Ticks announce "{öğe} tamamlandı" politely, once.
- **Hints:** non-modal and they do not steal focus. They are reachable with F6 (landmark cycling) and listed in
  Yardım › Neler yeni. Strips are `role="status"` and announced once.
- **Language and text:**
  - Every string is TR first with an EN key, with no raw keys or enums.
  - Copy is plain and second-person formal ("siz"). Sentences are ≤ 20 words.
  - Never colour alone: done = check icon + the word "tamamlandı".
- **Contrast:** everything is AA in light and dark. The ring is AAA against the background (tokens.md).
  Forced-colours: the ring uses `Highlight`, and the scrim is replaced by an outline on the card.
- **Touch and zoom:** 44 px targets and 16 px inputs. Nothing is hover-only. The sheet and tour work at 200 % zoom
  and 320 px width (WCAG 1.4.10): cards stack and the step card becomes a docked sheet under 640 px.

## 15. Acceptance checks (per ticket in the ROADMAP; summary)

1. A new academician signs in on a phone and sees the sheet once.
   - "Sonra" means it is gone on reload and on another device.
   - It comes back 3 days later, at most twice.
2. The same account on a desktop after "Bitir" in the tour: no sheet, and the checklist shows the same progress.
3. Granting `approvals.decide` to a staff member shows the delta sheet with only the approver card.
4. An e-mail deep link to `/approvals/123` is never interrupted.
5. `/public/**`, `/setup` and `/welcome` never call `GET /welcome`.
6. Reduced motion: no transform animation, the Lottie poster is shown, and every state is reached.
   Keyboard-only: the full tour, Esc and focus return work.
7. Admin: switching "Karşılama rehberi" off hides the sheet at the next load. Editing a TR text shows it to that
   audience. "Show again" re-shows it with "Güncellendi". Stats hide groups under 5.
8. MOBILE M-01/02/03/05 pass with the sheet, tour and checklist open at 390×844, tr-TR.
